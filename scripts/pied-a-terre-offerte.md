## Phase 2 — Native booking & Guesty · € 4,000

**Goal:** the full booking journey on your domain, kept in sync with Guesty for calendar, prices, and your booking channels.

| Area | What is delivered |
|---|---|
| **Search & availability** | Date/guest selection, real-time availability and pricing via Guesty |
| **Checkout** | Branded step flow on the site (details, terms, summary) without sending guests to an external portal |
| **Payment** | Your chosen payment method, connected to Guesty and the checkout on your site |
| **Confirmation** | A clear **branded confirmation page** after booking, plus a **branded confirmation email** that matches the website — professional, on-brand, easy for guests to understand (not a generic Guesty-style message) |

**Phase 2 dependencies (on your side):** active Guesty account, payment set up, and clear rules for rates and fees. I build the booking experience; you provide Guesty and payment access and the business rules.
